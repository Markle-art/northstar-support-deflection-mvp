 Customer Journey

1. Order Status

Customer → Selects Track My Order → Enters the order number → Receives the order status.

If the order is not found, the customer receives a helpful message explaining what to check and what to do next.

2. Stock Availability

Customer → Searches for a product → Receives the product availability.

If no matching product is found, the customer receives helpful feedback suggesting they check the product name or search for a similar product.

 Improvements to Wording and Interface

1. Use clear buttons such as **“Track My Order”** and **“Check Stock Availability.”**
2. Give simple instructions explaining what information the customer needs to enter.
3. Use clear status messages such as “Order Found”**, **“Order Not Found”, “In Stock”, and “Out of Stock.”
4. Provide helpful next steps when an order or product cannot be found.
5. Keep the interface simple, with fewer steps and easy-to-understand language.
