# Pitch Script — The Northstar Sprint

## Speaker Assignments

| Order | Speaker | Section | What They Explain | Handoff Line |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mark Ochieng (Product Lead) | Problem | The support ticket pain point, who's affected, why it matters | "...so we designed a way for customers to get these answers themselves. Here's what that experience looks like." |
| 2 | Grace Murigi (CX Lead) | Customer Experience | Walks through the journey map — how a customer moves through the widget, including the honest fallback path | "That experience needed to actually be built — here's how." |
| 3 | Mark Kamaamia (Technical Lead) | Solution & Demo | What was built, the tech choice (simple, static, transparent), then runs the live demo | "Of course, we didn't just build it — we tested it." |
| 4 | Loureen Shillah (QA Lead) | Testing & Evidence | Test coverage, what was tested, results, any bugs found and fixed | "And all of this needs to be packaged for a real handoff — that's where delivery comes in." |
| 5 | Maureen Naranoi (Project & Delivery Lead) | Go-Live & Next Steps | Readiness note summary — what works, limitations, what Northstar would need to go further | (closing) |

---

## 3-Minute Version
Each speaker gets ~30–35 seconds. Cut to one key sentence per section:
1. **Mark Ochieng:** State the problem in one sentence + who it affects
2. **Grace Murigi:** One sentence on the journey + fallback honesty
3. **Mark Kamaamia:** 60–90 seconds — brief tech explanation (10s) + live demo (50–80s)
4. **Loureen Shillah:** One sentence — "X test cases run, Y passed, all core paths verified"
5. **Maureen Naranoi:** One sentence on what works + one on next steps — close

## 5-Minute Version
Each speaker gets ~45–60 seconds, demo gets a bit more room:
1. **Mark Ochieng (45s):** Problem, ticket volume pain point, target categories chosen and why
2. **Grace Murigi (45s):** Full journey walkthrough with the fallback path highlighted as a deliberate design choice
3. **Mark Kamaamia (90–120s):** Tech stack choice and why it's appropriately simple, then live demo of both flows + one fallback case
4. **Loureen Shillah (45s):** Test approach, coverage, results summary, mention of any bug caught and fixed
5. **Maureen Naranoi (45–60s):** Readiness note summary, limitations disclosed honestly, next steps, close

## Demo Script (for Mark Kamaamia's section)
1. "This is QuickAnswer — a simple widget for two of Northstar's most common ticket types."
2. Click **Track My Order** → enter `#1004` → "Here's a live status lookup — shipped, with a date."
3. Click **Anything else?** → click **Check Stock** → enter `Wireless Headphones` → "Same pattern, different data — low stock, with a restock date."
4. Click **Anything else?** → click **Track My Order** → enter `#9999` → "And when we don't have an answer, we say so honestly and hand off to a real agent — we're not pretending to solve everything."
5. "All of this runs on a simple, static dataset — no AI, no complex infrastructure — which is exactly what a first-pass MVP should be."

## Closing Statement (Maureen Naranoi)
"QuickAnswer isn't a finished product — it's proof that a small, honest, well-tested tool can take real pressure off Northstar's support team for two of their most repetitive ticket types. We've been clear about what it does, what it doesn't do yet, and what it would take to go further. Thank you."
