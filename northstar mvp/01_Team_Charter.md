# Team Charter — The Northstar Sprint

## Project
Support Deflection MVP for Northstar Retail Co. — a lightweight, rule-based tool that lets customers self-serve answers on **Order Status** and **Stock Availability**, reducing repetitive tickets for the human support team.

| Customer Experience Lead | Grace Murigi | Designs the customer journey, widget copy, and interaction flow |
| QA & Testing Lead | Loureen Shillah | Writes and executes test cases; logs bugs and results |
| Project & Delivery Lead | Maureen Naranoi | Owns the task board, audit trail, go-live note, and final packaging |

## Purpose
To design and demo a genuinely simple, working MVP that shows how Northstar Retail could reduce repetitive support tickets for at least two categories — while producing honest, traceable evidence of how a 5-person cross-functional team actually collaborated.

## Working Principles
1. **Simplicity over impressiveness.** We are not building a production system — we are proving a concept clearly and honestly.
2. **Every contribution is real and traceable.** No one "pretends" to do a task that belongs to someone else's role. No fabricated commits, timestamps, or results.
3. **No invented statistics.** We do not claim measured business impact we have not actually tested.
4. **Board reflects reality.** Status is updated the same day work happens — not batched at the end.
5. **Handoffs matter.** Product → CX → Technical → QA → Delivery is a real chain of dependencies, and each person's output feeds the next person's work.
