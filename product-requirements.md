Northstar Retail Co. — Product Requirements

1. Main Problem

Northstar Retail Co. is a mid-size e-commerce company whose support team is spending too much time handling repetitive customer questions. The main repetitive queries identified for this MVP are order-status and stock-availability questions.

Customers frequently contact support to ask where their order is, whether it has shipped, or whether a product is available. These questions can often be answered automatically without requiring a support agent.

The Support Deflection MVP aims to provide customers with quick self-service answers and reduce unnecessary manual ticket handling.

2. Order Status — Common Customer Questions

◇. Where is my order?
◇. Has my order shipped yet?
◇. What is the status of my order?
◇. When will my order arrive?
◇. Can I check the status of my order?

3. Stock Availability — Common Customer Questions
 ◇ Is this product in stock?
 ◇ Do you have this in a different color?"
 ◇ Is this item available?
 ◇ Do you have this product in another size?
 ◇ When will an out-of-stock product be available again?

4. What the MVP Should Do

The MVP should:

- Allow customers to look up an order using an order number.
- Provide the available status of the order.
- Allow customers to search for products by name.
- Show whether a matching product is available.
- Support partial product-name searches where possible.
- Provide a clear fallback message when an order or product cannot be found.
- Reduce the need for customers to contact a human support agent for these common questions.
- Provide a simple and easy-to-understand customer experience.

5. What the MVP Does NOT Need to Do

The MVP does not need to:

- Process actual payments.
- Create or cancel orders.
- Process actual refunds or returns.
- Connect to Northstar's live inventory or order-management systems.
- Provide real-time delivery tracking.
- Replace the entire customer support team.
- Handle every possible customer-support question.
- Be a production-ready commercial system.

This is a proof-of-concept designed to demonstrate that repetitive support questions can be deflected through self-service.

6. How This Could Reduce Support Tickets

By allowing customers to independently check their order status and product availability, the MVP can answer common questions before a support ticket needs to be created.

This can reduce the number of repetitive tickets reaching support agents, allowing them to spend more time on complex customer issues that require human assistance.

The MVP therefore demonstrates a practical approach to reducing manual support workload while improving the speed of responses to customers.
